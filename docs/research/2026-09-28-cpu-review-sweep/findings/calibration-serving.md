## CALIB-1: TemperatureScaler's fixed-step gradient descent does not converge: the served temperatures are not the NLL minimiser (they stay near 1 for this model's small logits)

- **Severity:** P1. **Status:** confirmed. **Type:** bug. **CPU cost:** instant. **Placement:** new item CPU-04.
- **Files:** `src/neural_trade/calibration/temperature_scaling.py`, `src/neural_trade/calibration/pipeline.py`, `tests/test_calibration.py`

**Description.** _fit_temperature (temperature_scaling.py:62-106) fits T with 500 steps of plain gradient descent from T=1, with lr=0.05. The gradient is dNLL/dT = mean((p-y)(-z/T^2)), which scales with the logit variance and with 1/T^2. For small logits, or for any T far above 1, the total movement 500*0.05*|grad| is far too small to reach the optimum. The loop has no convergence test and no upper bound. The best-T bookkeeping is also one step off: nll is computed on base_logits/T_old (line 91, used at line 102), but best_T stores T_new (lines 99-104). This project's direction heads have P(up) s.d. of about 0.02-0.03 (runs/experiments/direction_v1/REPORT.md:52), so the logit s.d. is about 0.1. In that regime the fitted T stays in [0.86, 1.05] whatever the true temperature is, from 0.33 to 17. Every gate run's temperatures are in [0.94, 1.03]: m3 1.012/0.991/0.951, m4 0.997/0.998/0.981, m5 1.024/0.940/1.013, m6 1.009/0.985/1.033 (runs/gates/m*/analytics.json 'temperature'). These are consistent with an optimiser that barely moves, not with heads that are well calibrated. Calibrated P(up) is the served p_up_calibrated. It feeds SignalFrame (the agreement and consensus votes at 0.55/0.45), threshold_spike (0.45/0.55), enhanced_multi_horizon and liberal. It is also used for calibrated ECE/Brier and for the reliability diagrams. The context of D-009 ('fixed 0.55/0.45 lines are rarely crossed by calibrated P(up)') rests on these probabilities. AUC and MCC at 0.5 are unaffected, because T > 0 keeps the order and the sign of the logits.

**Failure scenario.** Synthetic calibration block with 20,000 samples, logit s.d. 0.1 and labels drawn from sigmoid(z/T*). For T* = 0.33 the fit gives T = 0.865 (the exact MLE is 0.329). For T* = 5 it gives T = 1.054 (MLE 17.36). The project's own test fixture (tests/test_calibration.py, SHARPEN=3, so the true T is 3) gets T = 2.41 on every horizon (MLE 2.99). The test still passes, because it asserts only that the probabilities move toward 0.5. Real BTC data (CI-style smoke: 1 epoch, 2000 sequences): the served h1 temperature is 3.40 with a cal NLL of 0.806, while the NLL minimum is 0.693, reached at the search bound (the head has no skill). For h0, T = 2.51 with NLL 0.744 against 0.693.

**Evidence (finder, reproduced).** Reproduced. (1) agents/calibration-serving/t_temp.py compares _fit_temperature with scipy minimize_scalar on log T. Output rows (logit_sd, true_T, GD_T, MLE_T): 0.10 0.33 0.8646 0.3292; 0.10 0.50 0.9443 0.5293; 0.10 2.00 1.0223 1.6036; 0.10 5.00 1.0542 17.3600; 1.00 5.00 1.9616 4.4532 (NLL 0.6964 vs 0.6870); 3.00 5.00 2.9121 4.9887. (2) t_temp2.py on the test_calibration _synthetic fixture: fitted T = 2.412/2.396/2.412, exact MLE 2.987/2.966/3.011. (3) t_smoke.py: train_and_evaluate for 1 epoch on the first 4000 CSV rows with MAX_SEQUENCE_COUNT=2000 (51 s). Cal block: h0 T_saved 2.506, T_mle 1000 (at the bound), nll_T1 0.881, nll_gd 0.744, nll_mle 0.693. h1: T_saved 3.405, nll_gd 0.806, nll_mle 0.693. h2: 1.420 against 1.526. (4) Candidate fix: bounded minimize_scalar on log T in [0.01, 100], saved as agents/calibration-serving/candidate_fix_temperature_scaling.py. It reproduces the MLE exactly on all rows, and tests/test_calibration.py passes (10 passed in 0.62 s).

**Verifier (reproduced): confirmed.** Code (head 1a2457a; code same as 7785ec9): temperature_scaling.py:87-106 runs 500 fixed steps of GD from T=1 with lr=0.05. There is no convergence test and no upper bound (only T>=1e-3). The best-T bookkeeping is one step off: the NLL at line 102 is taken on logits/T_old (line 91), but best_T at line 104 stores T_new (line 99). pipeline.py:209-213 calls fit with the defaults. Pure numpy repro, no training: agents/verify-calibration-serving/v_temp.py, n=20,000. Columns are (logit sd, true T, GD T, exact MLE T via bounded minimize_scalar): 0.1 0.33 0.8646 0.3292 | 0.1 0.50 0.9443 0.5293 | 0.1 2 1.0223 1.6036 | 0.1 5 1.0542 17.36 | 1 2 1.6181 2.0260 | 1 5 1.9891 4.8678 | 3 5 2.8680 4.7621. In the 0.33 row the calibrated P(up) s.d. is 0.029 with GD and 0.074 at the MLE. With the same data, GD reaches the MLE only after 5,000 steps (0.3511). The test_calibration fixture (SHARPEN=3) fits T=2.412/2.396/2.412 against MLE 2.987/2.967/3.011, and the test still passes. Gate temperatures (runs/gates/m*/analytics.json): m3-m6 lie in [0.9397, 1.0333]; m1a/m1b/m2 are 1.30-1.90. Not re-run: the finding's 1-epoch real-BTC smoke (training is banned under the FAST CPU RULES). The verdict rests on the numpy proof.

**Verifier corrections.** (a) The gate claim covers m3-m6 only, range [0.940, 1.033]. m1a/m1b/m2 have T 1.30-1.90, which fits GD moving only when logits are large. (b) Acceptance (1) as written cannot be checked. At n=20,000 the MLE itself misses the true T by up to 12% (logit sd 1, T*=5) and up to 99% (sd 0.1, T*=5) over 5 seeds (v_temp_acc.py). Replace it with: on every (sd in {0.1,1,3}) x (T* in {0.33,0.5,2,5}) block, the fitted T's NLL is within 1e-6 of a bounded scalar search. Keep true-T recovery within 10% only for sd in {1,3} and T* in {0.33,0.5,2} (max measured MLE error 5.8%). Add a discriminating row: sd 0.1, T*=0.33, n=20,000, seed fixed gives T < 0.45 (today 0.865). Acceptance (2) |T-3|<0.15 holds with the fix (max deviation 0.034). (c) Also fix the misleading summary label: pipeline.py:457-462 prints 'well-calibrated (T ~ 1)' for T in [0.95, 1.05]. (d) scripts/golden_run.py:53-62 records raw predictions and coverage/width but neither the temperatures nor the calibrated P(up). So golden verify neither shows this fix nor guards T against a module move. Add temperature/{h} and the calibrated direction_prob to the recording. (e) Timing: MVP growth point 2 (control panel and comparison: the scorer's calibrated_quantile backtest and the calibrated ECE/Brier). Land it before NT-031/NT-050 record leaderboard numbers, as its own number-changing commit with a new golden recording, never inside a D-023 move. The before/after trade-count check on the reference run goes to the owner, as the finding says. cpu_cost instant.

**Backlog check (finder).** Not covered. BACKLOG NT-001..NT-025, ROADMAP, STATUS and REMEDIATION_PLAN_2026-09 never mention temperature convergence. NT-003 is about direction skill (AUC/MCC), which T does not change. NT-018(4) is about ECE binning in the refit table, not about the fitted T.

**Fix sketch.** Replace the GD loop with a bounded 1-D search on log T. Use scipy.optimize.minimize_scalar(method='bounded', bounds=(log 0.01, log 100), xatol=1e-6); scipy is already a declared dependency. Another option is Newton's method on log T with a convergence tolerance. Keep n_steps and lr in the signature (they become unused), or deprecate them. When the optimum lands on a bound, log it and record it in temperature_params.json (for example 'at_bound': {h: true}), so that a no-skill head reads as 'flattened', not as calibrated. Fix the best_T bookkeeping or remove it. Tighten the test so that it pins T against the MLE. The changed calibrated P(up) moves the fixed-threshold strategies, so the lead should route a before/after trade-count check on the reference run through the owner, as for NT-007.

**Acceptance (proposed).** (1) A new test in tests/test_calibration.py: on synthetic blocks (n >= 20,000) with logit s.d. in {0.1, 1, 3} and true T in {0.33, 0.5, 2, 5}, TemperatureScaler.fit returns a T whose NLL is within 1e-6 of a bounded scalar search, and within 5% of the true T wherever the MLE is inside the bounds. (2) test_fit_apply_coverage_temperature_and_round_trip asserts |T_h - 3| < 0.15 for SHARPEN=3. (3) T is bounded, and a fit that hits a bound is flagged in temperature_params.json (test). (4) tests/test_calibration.py, tests/test_calibration_pass.py and tests/test_predictor_round_trip.py pass, and ruff is clean.

## CALIB-2: predict_last / 'neural-trade predict --last' bypass Config.PREPROCESSORS: wrong forecast with RESAMPLE_MINUTES > 1, unsorted CSVs or a trailing empty close

- **Severity:** P2. **Status:** confirmed. **Type:** bug. **CPU cost:** instant. **Placement:** new item CPU-15.
- **Files:** `src/neural_trade/serving/predictor.py`, `src/neural_trade/cli.py`, `tests/test_predictor_round_trip.py`

**Description.** cmd_predict --last (cli.py:93-95) takes the raw 'close' column of the CSV as it stands and passes it to Predictor.predict_last (predictor.py:115-122). predict_last feeds close[-LOOKBACK:] straight to the model. The frame path (predict_frame / predict_windows_frame, predictor.py:124-152) and training (DataProcessor.preprocess, processor.py:82-86) both run Config.PREPROCESSORS first: standardize_ohlcv, sort_dedupe, resample_bars, drop_missing_close. So the newest-window forecast is computed on different bars from the ones the model was trained on whenever the CSV is not already clean, sorted, 1-minute data at RESAMPLE_MINUTES. The realised-vol conformal scale is then computed on the wrong bar spacing as well. RUNBOOK.md:57 documents this command as how to forecast with a saved bundle. The bundled CSV is clean (monotonic, no duplicates, no gaps, checked), so the default path is unaffected. RESAMPLE_MINUTES is a supported Config field, however, and any other data source can trigger the bug.

**Failure scenario.** The same bundle (untrained, gru_attention, seed 0) on 3000 one-minute bars. (a) With RESAMPLE_MINUTES=5, predict_last(raw closes) gives h1 delta -75.18 and sigma 199.86. predict_frame gives -281.24 and sigma 207.23 for the same newest bar (index 2025-10-13 04:25). The model saw 60 one-minute bars instead of 60 five-minute bars. (b) A newest-first CSV makes predict_last forecast from the OLDEST 60 bars: last_close 110011.06, while the true newest close is 101387.49. h1 delta -370.53 against -75.18. (c) A trailing candle with an empty close returns a plausible neutral forecast (delta 3.2 = pred_mean, p_up 0.5, sigma 261.0) with last_close NaN. The CLI prints this as JSON containing bare NaN tokens, which is not valid JSON.

**Evidence (finder, reproduced).** Reproduced with agents/calibration-serving/t_pred.py (resample: 11.8 s; unsorted: 19.9 s) and t_pred3.py. They build an untrained bundle with Models.build + ArtifactBundle.save, load it with Predictor.from_artifacts, and compare predict_last with predict_frame(frame).iloc[-1]. Observed outputs are quoted in failure_scenario. tests/test_predictor_round_trip.py:88-96 compares predict_last with predict_frame only on sorted 1-minute synthetic bars at RESAMPLE_MINUTES=1.

**Verifier (reproduced): confirmed.** Code: cli.py:93-95 passes the raw CSV 'close' column to predict_last. predictor.py:115-118 feeds close[-LOOKBACK:] to predict() with no preprocessing. predict_frame and predict_windows_frame (predictor.py:124-152) and training (data/processor.py:82-86) run Config.PREPROCESSORS first. Reproduced without any Keras model or inference: agents/verify-calibration-serving/v_serving.py uses Predictor(bundle, model=FakeModel), a deterministic numpy stand-in for the 10 heads, and a spy that captures the window fed to predict(). Clean 1-min bars: windows equal, h1 delta -10.32 = -10.32. RESAMPLE_MINUTES=5: window differs, h1 delta -10.32 vs 7.72 and p_up_cal 0.3505 vs 0.5120, though last_close is equal. Newest-first CSV: last_close 110005.03 (the oldest bar) vs 105923.47. Duplicate last timestamp: the window differs (28.41 vs 28.10). Trailing empty close: predict_last returns last_close NaN, p_up 0.5, and lo90/hi90 NaN. json.dumps writes a bare NaN token, and a strict parse (parse_constant raising) fails on 'NaN'. The whole script takes 5.3 s. Not covered by any NT item. NT-041(8) adds gap handling to windowing, and NT-053 notes predict_last's API change for window-free, but neither covers the preprocessing bypass.

**Verifier corrections.** Line refs: the RUNBOOK command is now docs/RUNBOOK.md:91 (was :57), and the round-trip test is tests/test_predictor_round_trip.py:85-96. The finding's delta values (-75.18/-281.24, delta 3.2 = pred_mean) come from an untrained Keras model and were not re-run (inference is banned). With the numpy stand-in, the trailing-NaN case gives delta NaN, not pred_mean. The exact neutral numbers depend on the model, but the defect (a garbage row plus invalid JSON) is the same. Fix as sketched: route --last through the preprocessed frame, for example predict_windows_frame(df) followed by the last row, and drop or refuse non-finite windows. A fake-model test (as in v_serving.py) checks parity without Keras in about 5 s. MVP growth point 4 (generality: NT-041 bar sizes make RESAMPLE_MINUTES != 1 a normal setup). Land it before or with NT-041. It also eases NT-047, whose OHLCV input predict_last(close) cannot carry.

**Backlog check (finder).** Not covered. No BACKLOG, ROADMAP, STATUS or REMEDIATION item mentions predict_last, --last or serving preprocessing.

**Fix sketch.** Serve the newest window from the preprocessed frame. In cmd_predict --last, call predictor.predict_frame(df).iloc[[-1]]; or add Predictor.predict_last_frame(frame), which runs validate_ohlcv_frame(run_preprocessors(frame, config)) and predicts only the last window from make_inference_windows. Keep predict_last(close), but document that it takes preprocessed closes at RESAMPLE_MINUTES. Make predict and predict_last raise ValueError on non-finite windows instead of sanitising them into a neutral forecast.

**Acceptance (proposed).** (1) A fast test builds an untrained bundle and checks that the --last output equals predict_frame(frame).iloc[-1] within rtol 1e-4 for: RESAMPLE_MINUTES=5, a reversed (newest-first) frame, and a frame with a duplicated timestamp. (2) A window with a non-finite close raises ValueError, or the row is dropped as drop_missing_close does (test). (3) 'neural-trade predict --last' prints strict JSON: json.loads(out, parse_constant=raise) succeeds (test). (4) tests/test_predictor_round_trip.py and tests/test_cli.py pass.

## CALIB-3: The serving path never loads plugins: a bundle trained with a PLUGINS_DIR component cannot be served (ComponentNotFoundError)

- **Severity:** P2. **Status:** confirmed. **Type:** bug. **CPU cost:** instant. **Placement:** new item CPU-16.
- **Files:** `src/neural_trade/serving/predictor.py`, `src/neural_trade/cli.py`, `src/neural_trade/training/artifacts.py`, `src/neural_trade/notebook/backtest_ui.py`

**Description.** cmd_train calls registries.load_all(cfg, plugins_dir=cfg.PLUGINS_DIR) before training (cli.py:56-62). Predictor.from_artifacts (predictor.py:76-78), ArtifactBundle.load / build_model (artifacts.py:95-119), cmd_predict (cli.py:86-91), cmd_backtest (cli.py:106-113) and notebook.backtest_ui.load_run_blocks never call load_all. The plan requires plugins to be 'wired only through registries.load_all(config) at Trainer/Predictor/CLI startup' (REMEDIATION_PLAN_2026-09.md:144). A preprocessor, model or layer registered by a plugin therefore exists at training time and is missing at serving time. The bundle's config.yaml keeps PLUGINS_DIR, so the fix has everything it needs.

**Failure scenario.** A plugin in <dir>/plugins/tag_pre.py registers Preprocessors 'noop_plugin_step'. Config(PLUGINS_DIR=<dir>/plugins, PREPROCESSORS=[...defaults..., 'noop_plugin_step']). After load_all (as cmd_train does), a bundle is saved. In a fresh process, 'python -m neural_trade.cli predict --artifacts <dir>/artifacts --csv bars.csv' fails with: neural_trade.core.exceptions.ComponentNotFoundError: Preprocessors: 'noop_plugin_step' not found. Available: [add_time_features, drop_missing_close, log_returns, resample_bars, sort_dedupe, standardize_ohlcv, strip_currency_symbols]. A plugin MODEL_NAME or layer fails the same way, in build_model.

**Evidence (finder, reproduced).** Reproduced with agents/calibration-serving/t_plugin_build.py (builds and saves the bundle, no training) followed by the CLI predict in a new process; the traceback is quoted above. Calling load_all(b.config, plugins_dir=b.config.PLUGINS_DIR, strict=False) before Predictor(b) makes predict_frame work: 'loaded plugin neural_trade_plugins.tag_pre', 441 rows.

**Verifier (reproduced): confirmed.** grep: load_all(..., plugins_dir=...) is called only in cli.py:62 (cmd_train) and cli.py:149 (registry). It is absent from Predictor.from_artifacts (predictor.py:76-78), ArtifactBundle.load/build_model (artifacts.py:95-119), cmd_predict/cmd_backtest (cli.py:86-114), notebook/backtest_ui.load_run_blocks (:239-247) and notebook/session.py. Reproduced without training or inference. agents/verify-calibration-serving/v_plugin_save.py registers the plugin preprocessor 'noop_plugin_step' from plug/plugins/tag_pre.py via load_all (strict), then saves a bundle whose config names it. In a fresh process, v_plugin_load.py runs ArtifactBundle.load, then Predictor(bundle, model=FakeModel).predict_frame. That fails with ComponentNotFoundError: Preprocessors: 'noop_plugin_step' not found. Available: [add_time_features, drop_missing_close, log_returns, resample_bars, sort_dedupe, standardize_ohlcv, strip_currency_symbols]. After load_all(b.config, plugins_dir=b.config.PLUGINS_DIR, strict=False), predict_frame gives 441 rows. In the same fresh process, DataProcessor(cfg).preprocess, the path that train_and_evaluate and the notebook session use, fails the same way.

**Verifier corrections.** The gap is wider than serving. train_and_evaluate never loads plugins; only cmd_train does, before calling it. So notebook-session training (notebook/session.py) and the scripts fail with PLUGINS_DIR too. Put the load in shared startup: train_and_evaluate (or DataProcessor/Config resolution) plus ArtifactBundle.load/Predictor.from_artifacts, with strict=False and an error that names the directory when PLUGINS_DIR is missing. Resolve a relative PLUGINS_DIR to an absolute path when the bundle is saved, or record it relative to the bundle, because serving from another cwd breaks otherwise. Add to acceptance: a fresh process with Config(PLUGINS_DIR=tmp, PREPROCESSORS=[..., plugin]) runs DataProcessor(cfg).preprocess without an explicit load_all (test, no training). MVP growth point 1 (structure; registries and layering, NT-027/NT-046). Land it during MVP-1, before NT-046's Indicators registry invites plugin families.

**Backlog check (finder).** Not covered. No backlog item concerns plugin loading on the serving path. D-002 (all nine registries wired into the training path) is consistent with this fix.

**Fix sketch.** In Predictor.from_artifacts, or in ArtifactBundle.load, call registries.load_all(bundle.config, plugins_dir=bundle.config.PLUGINS_DIR, strict=False) before build_model. Log a clear error when PLUGINS_DIR no longer exists. cmd_predict, cmd_backtest and load_run_blocks then inherit the fix.

**Acceptance (proposed).** (1) A fast test writes a tmp plugin preprocessor, saves an untrained bundle whose config names it, and runs 'neural-trade predict' in a subprocess; the command exits 0 and prints the frame. (2) A bundle whose PLUGINS_DIR is missing fails with a message that names the missing directory (test). (3) tests/test_cli.py passes.

## CALIB-5: ConformalRegressor clamps the conformal rank to N and silently under-covers when ceil((1-alpha)(N+1)) > N; alpha is not validated

- **Severity:** P3. **Status:** confirmed. **Type:** bug. **CPU cost:** instant. **Placement:** new item CPU-25.
- **Files:** `src/neural_trade/calibration/conformal.py`

**Description.** predict_interval and empirical_quantile (conformal.py:171-175 and 185-191) compute k = ceil((1-alpha)(N+1)) and then take scores[min(k, N) - 1]. When k > N, that is alpha < 1/(N+1), split conformal's valid answer is an infinite interval. The code instead returns the largest calibration score, whose marginal coverage is only N/(N+1) < 1 - alpha. Yet the docstrings promise 'Guaranteed marginal coverage: P(y_true in [lo, hi]) >= 1 - alpha' (lines 157 and 41). alpha is not validated either: alpha = 1 gives k = 0, then index -1, which is the maximum score, the widest interval for the loosest request. Negative alpha is clamped silently. The module docstring's formula is also mis-parenthesised: 'ceil((1-alpha)*(N+1)/N)' at line 20, where the code correctly uses ceil((1-alpha)(N+1))/N. For the default cal blocks (thousands of samples) and the notebook slider (alpha >= 0.02), the clamp is not reachable. It is reachable through the API with tiny blocks or small alpha: the CI-sized cal block has 132 sequences, so any alpha < 0.0075 triggers it.

**Failure scenario.** N=5 exchangeable N(0,1) scores, alpha=0.1: empirical coverage over 4000 trials is 0.830, at a 0.90 target (k=6 > 5). N=8: 0.887 (k=9 > 8). The interval is returned with no warning. With N >= 9 the coverage is correct: 0.899-0.905 for N in {9, 19, 50, 200, 1000}.

**Evidence (finder, reproduced).** Reproduced with agents/calibration-serving/t_conf.py, part (a). Output: 'N=5 coverage=0.8297 theory=0.8333 k=6 CLAMPED', 'N=8 coverage=0.8872 theory=0.8889 k=9 CLAMPED', 'N=9 coverage=0.8990', 'N=1000 coverage=0.8962 (theory 0.9001)'. **Verifier (reproduced): confirmed.** conformal.py:173-175 and :190 clamp k=ceil((1-alpha)(N+1)) to N. The module docstring at line 20 is mis-parenthesised, while the code comment at line 172 is right. agents/verify-calibration-serving/v_conf.py, 4000 exchangeable N(0,1) trials at alpha=0.1: N=5, k=6 CLAMPED, coverage 0.8297; N=8, k=9 CLAMPED, 0.8872; N=9, 0.8990; N=19, 0.9052; N=200, 0.9040. alpha is not validated. Reachability: the notebook slider is limited to alpha in [0.02, 0.4] (calibration_ui.py:229). 'neural-trade predict --alpha' and the API take any float.

**Verifier corrections.** Add the alpha > 1 case: a negative index silently returns an arbitrary lower score, for example alpha=1.5 gives the 5th of 10. So the fix serves MVP growth point 4 and should land before or with NT-041.

**Backlog check (finder).** Not covered. NT-014 and NT-015 concern bootstrap/HAC intervals of reported metrics, not the conformal quantile itself.

**Fix sketch.** In predict_interval and empirical_quantile, raise ValueError unless 0 < alpha < 1. When k > N, return +/-inf half-widths (or q = inf) and log a warning naming the N needed, ceil(1/alpha) - 1. Fix the docstring formula at line 20.

**Acceptance (proposed).** (1) ConformalRegressor fit on 5 scores with alpha=0.1 gives infinite bounds, or raises with a message naming the minimum N (test).
