## EVAL-1: Diebold-Mariano z of the CRPS/NLL baseline cells ignores vol-regime persistence: 26-42% false 'significant' verdicts at nominal 5%

- **Severity:** P1. **Status:** confirmed. **Type:** bug. **CPU cost:** short. **Placement:** new item CPU-08.
- **Files:** `src/neural_trade/evaluation/report.py`, `src/neural_trade/visualization/analytics_tables.py`, `tests/test_eval_metrics.py`

**Description.** dm_z (report.py:346-362) takes the long-run variance of the per-sample loss difference with a Bartlett kernel whose default lag is DM_LAG_PER_STEP x bars ahead = 20/30/40 bars (report.py:88, 359). That lag covers the h-1 bar overlap of the targets. It does not cover the autocorrelation that persistent forecasts create. Variance heads and realized-vol-like sigmas follow the vol regime (60-bar log realized vol of the bundled BTC data has autocorrelation 0.72 at lag 60, 0.49 at 200 and 0.31 at 600). The CRPS and NLL loss differences therefore stay autocorrelated far beyond 2h (their autocorrelation is still +0.05 to +0.09 at lag 300 on real data), and the long-run variance is underestimated. The report docstring (report.py:52-55) says 'volatility clusters beyond that' but keeps the fixed 2h lag. Every DM cell of the variance group (model vs const_var crps and nll, and the realized_vol rows that NT-013 plans) gets an inflated |z|. The markdown then prints 'beats (DM z +x)' where it should print 'noise'. STATUS cites exactly this number as evidence that the variance heads work ('CRPS DM z 6.2 / 4.0 / 4.0', M4). The delta and direction DM cells (rmse, mae, brier, acc) stayed near nominal (4-10%) in the same clean null. Not covered by NT-013/014/015: NT-013 only notes that one Brier cell depends on the lag and asks for a multiplicity note.

**Failure scenario.** Input: y = real BTC h-bar deltas (bundled CSV, random 7,236-row blocks = the test size). Forecasts A and B are two independent draws of one long-memory log-sigma process (x = (AR1(0.99) + AR1(0.999)) / sqrt 2, sigma = s0 exp(0.3 x)), both independent of y, so E[CRPS_A] = E[CRPS_B] exactly. A 5% test should reject 5% of the time. Output: report.dm_z rejects in 42/34/33% of the draws for CRPS and 36/28/26% for NLL (h = 10/15/20). Real-data example: a causal 60-bar realized-vol sigma against a constant sigma fit on the first 70% of the data, scored on the last 7,236 bars. The report's z is +3.71 (CRPS h0), +2.94 (NLL h0) and +2.09 (CRPS h1), all 'significant'. With a fixed-b bandwidth (lag 723) and the Kiefer-Vogelsang critical value, the same cells give 1.45 / 1.85 / 0.99 on the 1.96 scale, all noise.

## EVAL-2: Correction to NT-013: the var/err^2 Spearman boot_z (80-bar blocks) is ~2x too small under persistent sigma forecasts: 29-31% false 'beats' at nominal 5%

**Description.** The ranked baseline metrics get their noise flag from a paired moving-block bootstrap with BLOCK = 80 bars (report.py:86, 366-374, 474-475, 479-486, 996-998).
