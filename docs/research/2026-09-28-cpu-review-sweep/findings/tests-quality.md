## TEST-1: Leaderboard metric formulas have no unit test: a sign-flipped Sharpe, and a sharpe_net computed from gross returns, both pass the whole suite

- **Severity:** P1. **Status:** not verified. **Type:** test-gap. **CPU cost:** instant. **Placement:** new item CPU-09.
- **Files:** `src/neural_trade/strategy/performance.py`, `tests/test_backtest.py`

**Description.** No test checks strategy/performance.py against hand-computed values. That module holds sharpe (lines 12-16), sortino (19-22), max_drawdown (25-31), profit_factor (34-39) and summarize (42-59), and the leaderboard ranks on its sharpe_net (VISION, D-020). The only Sharpe assertion in the suite is `sharpe_net == 0` on a flat equity curve (tests/test_backtest.py:124), and a sign flip keeps that true. Line coverage is 96.4%, but nothing pins the formulas. I made 16 mutants of performance.py; 13 survived. Two of them survived the whole non-TF fast tier: `-r.mean()` in sharpe (so a loss ranks as a gain), and sharpe_net computed from bar_returns_gross (so the 'net after costs' column shows the gross Sharpe). The other 11 survived their related test set (see evidence). By code reading, the tf-marked modules and the slow tests assert nothing about Sharpe sign, net or gross, Sortino, profit factor, hit rate, turnover or holding time.

**Failure scenario.** On a synthetic 3,000-bar block where the default strategy calibrated_quantile makes 138 trades and loses money after costs: the baseline gives sharpe_net -85.70. The P1 mutant gives +85.70, which would rank the worst configuration first. The P10 mutant gives +176.61, the gross Sharpe shown as net. In both cases 565 of 565 selected tests pass.

**Evidence (finder, reproduced).** Harness in $AG=<scratch>/agents/tests-quality. `python scripts/mutate.py muts_full.json` runs pytest -n 2 over tests/ with -m 'not slow and not gpu and not tf', deselecting the three tests that train or build a model. Results: 'P1-sharpe-sign-FULL: SURVIVED 565 passed' and 'P10-sharpe-net-uses-gross-FULL: SURVIVED 565 passed'. Related-set runs (muts_backtest.json against test_backtest, test_notebook_signal_flags, test_notebook_ui, test_viz_trade_analytics, test_viz_trading, test_viz_noise_bands) also left these SURVIVED: P4 (Sortino on the upside), P7 (drawdown measured from the first bar), P8 (profit factor inverted), P9 (hit rate counts 0 P&L as a win), P11 (total_return from equity[1]), P12 (turnover counts one side), P13 (avg_hold + 1). P2, P3 and B22 (annualisation) also survived; NT-040 covers those. Only P5 and P6 (max-drawdown denominator and sign) were killed. `python scripts/impact.py muts_backtest.json P1-sharpe-sign P10-sharpe-net-uses-gross` printed: BASE sharpe_net=-85.701 sharpe_gross=+176.612; P1 sharpe_net=+85.701; P10 sharpe_net=+176.612. grep over tests/ finds no assertion on sortino, profit_factor value, hit_rate, turnover or avg_hold_bars computed from known inputs.

**Backlog check (finder).** NT-040 covers only the bar-size annualisation (periods_per_year). NT-031 tests the sort order of the leaderboard, not the metric it sorts on. NT-002 covers the null's position size. No item pins the formulas.

**Fix sketch.** Add tests/test_performance.py with hand-computed series: (a) returns [+1%, -1%, +2%] give sharpe = mean/std x sqrt(525,600) exactly, with the sign; (b) summarize on an equity curve with costs gives sharpe_net != sharpe_gross and each equals sharpe() of its own series; (c) Sortino uses only negative returns; (d) max drawdown of [100, 120, 90, 130, 104] is 0.25 (peak to trough, not from the start); (e) profit factor, hit rate with a 0-P&L trade, turnover = 2 x notional / initial equity, avg_hold. Pure numpy and training-free. MVP growth point 2 (leaderboard and comparison): land it before NT-026's scorer and NT-031, and before NT-040 edits performance.py.

**Acceptance (proposed).** (1) tests/test_performance.py exists and each of these mutants of strategy/performance.py makes it fail: Sharpe sign flip, sharpe_net from gross returns, Sortino on max(r,0), drawdown from equity[0], profit factor inverted, hit rate with >=, total_return from equity[1], turnover without the factor 2, avg_hold + 1 (checked by the implementer with a scripted mutation run, listed in the report).
