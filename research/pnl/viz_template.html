How to read it. The three panels share one time axis. On a computer, drag across the chart to zoom, double-click to reset and click to pin the readout. On a phone, swipe sideways to move in time, pinch to zoom and tap to read values; a vertical swipe scrolls the page. The buttons under the chart zoom and move on any device. Zoom below about four days and the price switches to 1-minute closes. A triangle marks each entry (up = long, down = short) at the minute of its fill, and a thin line runs to its exit. The position panel shows the exposure at each bar close, so a fill shows there at the end of its minute.

Numbers. PnL is cumulative net of fees and funding, compounded, from 0% on 2021-01-01; the sealed-year run starts flat and is chained onto the dev curve. Long ranges are drawn with the first, last, highest and lowest value of each screen pixel, so no spike is lost. The readout line uses the full data; when you point at a long range, the price is the last hourly close (its time is shown in brackets), and a click or tap pins that time and loads its 1-minute close. The dev return in the summary is the mean daily log return x 365. Gross and net bps per trade are realised PnL over equity at entry (size-weighted).
