How to read it. The three panels share one time axis: drag to zoom, double-click to reset, and the hover line reads all three panels at once (trade details join it below 60 days in view). Zoom below about four days and the price switches from hourly closes to 1-minute closes. Trade marks sit at the bar of the fill: a triangle for the entry (up = long, down = short) and a line to the exit.

Numbers. PnL is cumulative net of fees and funding, compounded, from 0% on 2021-01-01; the sealed-year run starts flat and is chained onto the dev curve. PnL points are hourly plus every fill; position points are exact at every fill (between fills, drift is drawn in steps of 0.01x). The dev return in the summary is the mean daily log return x 365. Gross and net bps per trade are realised PnL over equity at entry (size-weighted).

Source. Engine E re-simulation (research/pnl/viz_export.py); every run's daily PnL is bit-identical to the scored results in research/pnl/results/ (dev gates) and results/seal.json. Verdicts: plans/pnl_optimisation.md.
